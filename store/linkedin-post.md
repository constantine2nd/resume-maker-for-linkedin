## Post

Your LinkedIn profile is the source of truth. Your CV should be too.

Recruiters open your profile before the interview, often before they open your CV.
If the two disagree (an old job title, a missing project, different dates), they wonder which one is true.

So I stopped keeping a separate CV. I update my profile, and make the CV from it in one click.

I built a small free Chrome extension for that: Resume Maker for LinkedIn.
→ Open your profile, click "📄 Make CV"
→ Pick a template and accent color, switch sections on or off, edit any text in place
→ Print / Save as PDF

No account, no server, no tracking. Nothing leaves your browser, and the code is open source.

👉 https://constantine2nd.github.io/resume-maker-for-linkedin/
