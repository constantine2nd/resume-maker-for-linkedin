Turn your LinkedIn profile into a clean, professional resume (PDF) in one click.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Open your LinkedIn profile and click the "📄 Make CV" button.
2. Your resume opens in a new tab. Choose a template, an accent color and the sections you want.
3. Fix or add anything right on the page: click any text to edit it, add your email and phone, remove old jobs.
4. Click "Print / Save as PDF".

FEATURES
• Two templates: Classic (one column) and Sidebar (two columns)
• Your accent color, A4 or Letter paper, photo on or off
• Experience, education, certifications, projects, skills, languages and courses
• Full lists: the extension also reads your "Show all" pages (all skills, all projects…)
• Edit any text in place; your edits are kept
• Update from LinkedIn any time; if you have edited the resume, you choose what to keep
• Real text in the PDF (selectable and searchable), clean page breaks
• Move the button anywhere on the page, or hide it

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no server, no tracking, no ads
• Your profile is read only when you click the button
• Everything is stored only on your computer and never sent anywhere
• Open source (MIT): https://github.com/constantine2nd/resume-maker-for-linkedin

NOTES
• Works with LinkedIn's English interface.
• Email and phone are not read from LinkedIn (they are hidden behind "Contact info"); add them on the resume page.
