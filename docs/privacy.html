Privacy policy

Resume Maker for LinkedIn (Chrome extension) · Last updated: 18 November 2026

In short

The extension reads the LinkedIn profile page you have open, only when you click its “📄 Make CV” button, and turns it into a resume inside your browser. It stores that data only on your device. Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else. There are no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

What the extension reads

When you click “📄 Make CV” on a LinkedIn profile page (linkedin.com/in/…), the extension reads what that page and its “Show all” pages show: name, headline, location, website, profile photo link, About text, experience, education, certifications, projects, skills, languages and courses. It does not read other pages, your messages, your contacts or your LinkedIn account settings, and it does nothing until you click the button.

What it stores, and where

  • The profile data it read, your edited resume (including an email address or phone number if you type them in), your layout settings, and the position of the button.
  • All of this is kept in the extension's local storage (chrome.storage.local) on your computer. It is not synced to your Google account and not uploaded anywhere.
  • It stays until you change it, click “Reset to LinkedIn data”, or remove the extension; removing the extension deletes it.

What it sends

Nothing leaves your browser except normal traffic to LinkedIn itself:

  • To get complete lists, the extension opens your profile's “Show all” pages on linkedin.com (for example /details/skills/) in a hidden frame, just as if you opened them yourself.
  • The resume page shows your profile photo, which your browser loads directly from LinkedIn's image server (you can turn the photo off in the resume settings).

No data is sent to the developer or to any other server, and there is no analytics or tracking code.

Sharing and use

  • The data is not sold, shared with or transferred to third parties.
  • It is used only to create your resume, not for any other purpose.
  • It is not used to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

Permissions

  • storage: to keep your resume and settings on your device.
  • linkedin.com: to show the “📄 Make CV” button on profile pages and read the profile you have open when you click it.
