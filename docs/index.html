Resume Maker for LinkedIn

Turn your LinkedIn profile into a clean PDF resume in one click. Everything stays in your browser.

Install

How it works

  1. Open your LinkedIn profile and click 📄 Make CV.
  2. Pick a template and accent color, switch sections on or off, and fix any text in place (add your email and phone there).
  3. Click Print / Save as PDF.

Privacy

No account, no server, no tracking. Your profile is read only when you click the button and is stored only on your computer. Privacy policy
